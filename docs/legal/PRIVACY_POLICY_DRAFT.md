# Privacy Policy (draft)

**[Company name]**, [address], [email] ("we") runs Gymmie. Last updated: [date].

## Who we are to you
- **Gym owners and staff:** we are the provider of the service and the controller of your account data.
- **Gym members:** your gym decides what it records about you (membership, payments, attendance, health forms) and is responsible for it; we process it for the gym. Your training log in the member app is private to you.

## What we collect
- Account: name, phone number, email (optional), photo (optional), language.
- Gym records (entered by the gym): members' names, phones, birth dates, addresses, emergency contacts, health questionnaire answers (PAR-Q), memberships, payments, attendance, leads, products, staff.
- Member app: your training log (workouts, body weight, settings), your privacy and notification choices, devices signed in.
- Billing (gym owners): payment status and invoices. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store them.
- Technical: IP address and device name for security and sign-in lists; crash reports if enabled.

## Why
To provide the service, send sign-in codes and messages the gym asks us to send, bill gym owners, keep accounts secure, and meet legal duties (tax invoices).

## Who sees it
- A member's training log: only that member (and their trainer/gym staff if the member chooses to share a summary).
- A member's profile and health answers: the gym's owner and managers; a trainer only for members assigned to them and only the fields the member allows.
- Service providers: Razorpay (payments), [SMS provider], Meta (WhatsApp), [email provider], [hosting provider]. We do not sell personal data.

## Your choices
- Edit your profile and privacy settings in the app. Switch off optional notices (service, security and billing notices stay on).
- **Delete your account:** in the app (Settings > Delete my account) or at [website]/delete-account. For members this erases your training log, photo, contact and fitness details and preferences and ends your sign-ins; the gym keeps the records it must keep for its business (membership, payment and attendance history). Gym owners can ask us to close the gym and delete its data: [email].
- Ask for a copy, correction or erasure: [email]. Complaints: [grievance officer name and email].

## Keeping data
Active accounts: while the account exists. Closed gyms: deleted on request [or after N days]. Invoices: [N] years (tax law). Backups: deleted after 14 days.

## Security
Encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed one-time codes, role-based access, per-gym separation, automatic sign-out on revoked sessions.

## Children
Gyms may enrol members under 18 with a guardian's consent; the gym is responsible for obtaining it.

## Changes
We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
