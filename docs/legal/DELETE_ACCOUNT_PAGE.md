# Delete your Gymmie account (web page text)

Publish this at `[website]/delete-account`. Google Play requires such a page.

**In the app:** Settings > My account > Delete my account (members) or Settings > Delete my account (owners and staff). We send a code to your phone and you type DELETE.

**What is erased:** your name, phone number, email, photo, sign-ins and (members) your training log and preferences. **What the gym keeps:** the records it needs to run its business: membership, payment and attendance history, and invoices. Ask your gym if you want those removed.

**Gym owners:** closing a gym deletes all of its data and cannot be undone. Email [support email] from the phone number on the account and we will confirm and delete it.

**Cannot open the app?** Email [support email] with your phone number; we will verify it by sending a code.
