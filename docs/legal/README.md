# Legal drafts: have a lawyer review before you publish

These are starting points written to match what the app really does. They are **not legal advice**. Fill the `[brackets]`, publish them on your
website at `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund-and-cancellation` and `/delete-account`, and set `SITE_URL` in the build.

India: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies (consent, purpose limits, deletion, a grievance contact). Health data (PAR-Q) is sensitive.
